### How I lead product

- **Go to the site before writing the spec.** Watch what people actually do.
- **Price on value you can prove.** If the buyer can verify the saving, they'll share it.
- **Design for the worst connectivity.** Offline-first by default.
- **A metric without a baseline is a wish.**
- **Say what we won't build.** A scope is only as good as its non-goals.
- **Prototype before you persuade.** A working demo ends more debates than a deck.
